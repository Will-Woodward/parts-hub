# PARTS HUB launch checklist

## Business and compliance
- [ ] Confirm registration city/district and valid business address.
- [ ] Register the individual business under the real operator.
- [ ] Confirm approved scope covers the actual component sales and consignment model.
- [ ] Confirm tax registration, invoicing and bookkeeping requirements with local tax authority/accountant.
- [ ] Sign supplier consignment agreements defining title, authority to sell, pricing, settlement, stock accuracy, returns, warranty and loss/damage risk.
- [ ] Publish merchant identity, contact details, privacy policy, terms, delivery, returns/refunds and after-sales rules.
- [ ] Assess product-specific restrictions and import/export controls for listed components.

## Domain, hosting and filing
- [ ] Register a brand/domain after checking trademark and domain availability.
- [ ] Select a mainland cloud region after the operator and filing subject are confirmed.
- [ ] Complete ICP filing via the cloud provider's filing system before public launch on mainland hosting.
- [ ] Configure HTTPS, DNS, CDN, object storage, database backups and monitoring.

## Payments
- [ ] Apply for WeChat Pay website/Native and required JSAPI products under the registered merchant.
- [ ] Apply separately for Alipay PC website and mobile website payment products.
- [ ] Confirm approved business category, rates, settlement account and refund support.
- [ ] Store API keys/certificates only in server-side secret storage.
- [ ] Test signature verification, amount matching, idempotent notifications, refund and reconciliation.

## Operations
- [ ] Import initial 100 SKUs from a controlled spreadsheet template.
- [ ] Verify PN, manufacturer, date code, packaging, quantity and image accuracy.
- [ ] Test simultaneous orders against the same stock.
- [ ] Define consignment stock refresh and expiry process.
- [ ] Test low-stock, sold-out and manual hold states.
